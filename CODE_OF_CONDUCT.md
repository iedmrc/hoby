# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Hoby welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for impact.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Keep feedback focused on the work, not the person.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized attention.
- Trolling, personal attacks, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior conflicts with this code. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through an available private contact method on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/iedmrc). Do not put sensitive reports in a public issue.

This code applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project. It is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), available under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
